Research Focus

Probing the Standard Model & Beyond

My research is focused on developing a unified model that accommodates B anomalies, neutrino physics, dark matter phenomenology, the muon g−2 anomaly, and lepton flavor violating tau decays.

⚛️

Flavor Anomalies & SM Extensions

Rare leptonic and semileptonic B, D, and K meson decays. Beyond the Standard Model physics via leptoquarks, Z′ bosons, and vector-like fermions to explain RK, RD(*) anomalies observed at LHCb.

B-Physics Leptoquarks Z′
🌑

Dark Matter Model Building

Construction of BSM models with scalar, vector-like, and Majorana dark matter candidates. Connecting dark matter phenomenology to flavor anomalies and rare B decay signatures at colliders.

Scalar DM Majorana DM GeV-scale
🔬

Charged Lepton Physics

Physics analysis of the muon anomalous magnetic moment (g−2)μ at the J-PARC experiment. Electron dipole moment, and lepton flavor violating tau decays as probes of new physics.

Muon g−2 J-PARC LFV
🌌

Neutrino Physics

Neutrino mass generation with new BSM particles, modular symmetry approaches to the neutrino mass matrix, leptogenesis, and neutrino oscillation simulation using GLoBES.

Neutrino Mass Modular Symmetry GLoBES
